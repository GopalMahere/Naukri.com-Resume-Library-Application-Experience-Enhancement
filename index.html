The Internal Product Disconnect
What Naukri Tells Candidates to Do

Naukri Career Guidance advises tailoring resumes for each job application, and Naukri Campus Free Resume Maker explicitly allows creating multiple role-specific resume versions.

What Core Naukri Apply Actually Does

In the core job application flow, clicking Apply automatically submits the single resume uploaded to the main profile—without asking which version to use or recording which CV was sent.

03 · Clear Problem Definition (Strictly Separated from Solution)

What is actually broken in the Naukri.com application experience?

The Actual Problem: Job seekers often maintain multiple role-specific resumes (e.g., a Product Management CV and a Consulting CV). On Naukri.com, clicking Apply immediately submits the single resume stored on the main profile without asking which CV to use—forcing candidates to leave the job listing, overwrite their profile resume, and hunt for the same job again, while leaving no record of which CV went to which company.
P1

Application-Time Resume Friction

Single profile resume lock-in forces context switching
  • Clicking Apply proceeds with the single profile resume without prompting for CV selection.
  • Switching CVs forces a 5-step detour: exit job → open profile → replace CV → return to search → re-find the exact listing.
  • Listings can disappear or get buried during the detour, and the loop repeats on every role switch.
P2

Post-Application Resume Blindness

No application-wise record of which CV was sent
  • Candidates applying across tracks (e.g., Product Management & Consulting) overwrite their profile CV repeatedly.
  • When a recruiter calls days or weeks later, there is no application-level record showing which resume version was submitted.
  • Hurts interview preparation, forces manual external spreadsheets, and erodes candidate confidence.
Core Problem Statement

"Candidates do not have sufficient flexibility and control over resume selection and resume tracking throughout their job application journey."

08 · Product Architecture & Marketplace Guardrails

Designing for Naukri's two-sided marketplace without breaking Resdex

Because Naukri's core B2B revenue relies on recruiter resume search (Resdex), multi-resume support must protect recruiter signal quality while unlocking jobseeker control.

1. Resdex B2B Search Isolation

Exactly one CV is designated as the Primary Profile Resume indexed by Resdex for inbound recruiter searches. Secondary resumes remain private to outbound applications—preventing duplicate profiles or noisy recruiter search results.

2. Immutable Application Snapshots

Each application stores a read-only reference (`resume_version_id` + file snapshot). If a candidate later edits or deletes a CV from their Resume Library, their Applied Jobs history still shows the exact file sent to that recruiter.

3. Zero-Regression 1-CV Fast Path

Job seekers with only 1 uploaded resume bypass the picker modal entirely (keeping 1-click Apply speed intact). The lightweight bottom sheet/modal appears only when a user has ≥2 resumes saved.

4. Naukri 360 / AI Resume Maker Loop

Adds a "Save to Resume Library" action inside Naukri's AI Resume Builder (270K monthly downloads), converting AI-created resumes directly into selectable application assets and boosting B2C engagement.

09 · Prioritization & Phased Roadmap

De-risking delivery across three incremental phases

Sequenced by Impact vs. Effort: ship core friction removal first, layer application-wise tracking second, and unlock AI-driven role matching once multi-CV usage scales.

10 · Success Metrics & Experimentation Plan

How we measure success and protect core funnel conversion

Proposed KPIs and A/B test guardrails to validate both jobseeker utility and zero regression in Naukri's core Apply and Resdex funnels.

North Star Metric

Multi-Role Application Completion Rate

Percentage of Apply clicks resulting in completed submissions among users who browse ≥2 distinct job categories in a 14-day window.

Secondary Adoption KPIs
  • Library Adoption: % of active applicants saving ≥2 resumes.
  • Active Switching: % of applications submitted with a non-default CV.
  • Tracker Utility: Click-through rate on "View Submitted CV" inside Applied Jobs.
Guardrail Metrics
  • Apply Latency: Zero added friction or drop-off for single-CV users.
  • Recruiter Quality: Recruiter shortlist / CV download rate on Resdex & job responses.
  • Profile Churn: Reduction in repeated same-day profile CV overwrites.
Proposed A/B Experiment Design

Roll out behind a feature flag to a randomized treatment cohort of active jobseekers searching across 2+ role categories (Web + Android/iOS), measuring apply-to-submit conversion, profile-edit detours, and recruiter response quality against control over 4 weeks.
